Privacy,
without the fine print.

NetflixBD is designed as a lightweight title-discovery site. It has no accounts, first-party advertising, or analytics pixels.

What the site stores

The application has no user database or profile system. Search terms and title identifiers may appear in the page URL so navigation and shareable links work. The service worker may cache the application shell in your browser; you can clear it through your browser’s site-data controls.

External requests

To return search results, title details, and playback, your browser contacts IMDb through the configured CORS proxy, TMDB, Google Fonts, image hosts, and the selected video provider. These providers can receive ordinary request information such as your IP address, browser headers, requested URL, and referring page. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

Advertising and tracking

NetflixBD does not load first-party ad networks, Google AdSense, Google Analytics, or ShareThis. Playback and content displayed within the external player frame remain controlled by its provider and may follow that provider’s own advertising and privacy practices. NetflixBD does not sell personal information.

Changes and contact

This notice may change when the application’s services change. For questions, open an issue in the GitHub repository.

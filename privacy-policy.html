Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

NetflixBD is a static, open-source website available at https://netflixbd.pages.dev/. This policy explains what the site itself does and what happens when your browser contacts the external services needed for search, metadata, images, loading indicators, and video embeds.

Advertising and analytics

NetflixBD does not intentionally load first-party advertising, Google AdSense, Google Analytics, or ShareThis scripts. The site does not sell personal information.

Information processed by this site

The application has no account system, application database, or server-side user profile. Search text and selected title, season, and episode identifiers may appear in the page URL so links can be shared and browser navigation works.

External services

Your browser makes direct requests to services used by the application, including IMDb, TMDB, jsDelivr, the configured CORS proxy, and the selected third-party video provider. Those providers can receive ordinary request data such as your IP address, browser headers, requested URL, and referring page. A video loaded inside a cross-origin iframe is controlled by its provider and may have separate content, cookies, or privacy practices.

Local storage and caching

The Progressive Web App service worker uses the browser Cache API to cache the application shell. You can clear this data through your browser's site-data controls or by uninstalling the PWA.

Third-party policies

This policy does not govern external services. Review each provider's policy before using the site if you need more information about how that provider processes data.

Children's privacy

The site does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe project-controlled content creates a privacy concern, contact the maintainer through the repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its external services change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or project issues, use the GitHub issue tracker.
